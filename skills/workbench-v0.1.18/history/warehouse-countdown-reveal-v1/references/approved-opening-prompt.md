```text
Core action: two adult store employees must visibly and audibly count “Three, Two, One” together before lifting a white sheet. Both employees speak every number, both mouths match the words, and the sheet begins moving upward exactly on the final sound of “One.”

Generate a realistic vertical smartphone UGC video inside a bright warehouse superstore, recorded as one unbroken handheld take. The action follows this exact order: a customer reacts, the camera pans right, two employees perform the full countdown, they lift the sheet, and the product display is revealed.

The video opens in a wide store aisle under neutral, slightly cool overhead LED lights. On the left side of the frame, an adult male customer wearing a backward black cap, plain dark sunglasses, a loose gray T-shirt and black shorts holds an orange drink bottle. He notices something offscreen to the right, his eyes widen behind the glasses, his free hand rises to his mouth and he gives one short natural gasp. The bottle makes a small pendulum swing.

The handheld phone camera immediately follows his gaze with one fast horizontal pan to the right. Shelves, ceiling lights and pallet displays create natural horizontal motion blur only during the movement. The camera settles on a centered full-body view of two adult store employees, one man and one woman, standing behind a blue retail pallet. They wear simple solid-blue work polos and friendly natural smiles. A large opaque white fabric sheet completely covers a rectangular product display. Each employee uses both hands to hold the upper edge of the same sheet, keeping all four hands attached to the fabric.

Facing the camera, both employees count together in natural American English: “Three!” — “Two!” — “One!” Two distinct synchronized voices, one male and one female, are clearly audible at the same time. Both mouths visibly articulate every complete word. Each number forms one separate, evenly spaced beat: both employees make one small synchronized nod and one restrained downward elbow pulse, causing the top edge of the sheet to dip slightly and return. The sheet stays opaque and fully covers the products through all three beats.

On the final “n” sound of “One,” both employees immediately pull the sheet upward together. The products already physically exist beneath the fabric. The sheet rises as one continuous piece of real cloth with natural tension, wrinkles, weight and a small upward billow, revealing the display smoothly from bottom to top.

Reveal a clean, physically plausible retail display. Every visible product keeps the same shape, color, scale and perspective throughout the reveal. Product words, exact branding and graphic overlays are reserved for postproduction. End on a short stable hero hold: the product display is centered, both employees smile behind it with their arms raised, and the white sheet remains held above the display.

Use authentic modern phone-camera rendering, practical deep focus, realistic skin and fabric texture, clean stable product geometry, subtle handheld breathing and ordinary warehouse exposure. Audio consists of natural store ambience, the customer’s short gasp, the quick pan whoosh, both employees’ synchronized “Three, Two, One,” and the final cloth snap and rustle. Graphic text, captions and branding are added later in postproduction.
```
